Update Kami to Public

Connect your Kami by USB to install Public firmware v1.0.5.

  1. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer.
  2. Plug in your Kami with a USB data cable. Close other device tools.
  3. Connect, then choose Install Public. Keep it plugged in until verification finishes.

Your account, Wi-Fi, settings and SD files stay on the device.

Next time, update from your Kami itself. Public updates no longer need an OTA access key.

My device isn’t appearing

Try another USB data cable and port. Close other tabs or apps connected to the device. Use a desktop browser directly, not a browser inside another app. If connection still fails, ask support for your model’s boot-mode instructions.

What does this change?

This installs the verified Public v1.0.5 application on supported 16 MB Kami devices. It checks the existing memory layout first and never erases the whole device. Unsupported layouts stop before writing. Account pairing is preserved; a separate account-login problem may still need support.

If the cable disconnects during installation, reconnect and retry this page. Do not choose an erase-all or factory-reset tool.

Device contents are checked locally in your browser, never uploaded. This page does not request your account key or OTA key.
Steps to share

“Open this recovery link in Chrome or Edge on your computer. Plug in Kami, click Connect my Kami, select the USB device, then Install Public. Leave it connected until it says verified. Restart Kami and check Settings → About shows v1.0.5.”

Debug with Claude or Codex (optional)

Recovery runs entirely in this browser. If it fails, copy a short diagnostic summary for an agent to help troubleshoot. No account keys, Wi-Fi passwords or device files are included.
